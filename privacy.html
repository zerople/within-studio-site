Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Within Studio ("we") operates the channels The Wood Within and The House Within and the publishing tools that post our own videos to YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

What we collect

Our publishing tools connect only to accounts that we own. Through platform APIs (TikTok for Developers, Meta Graph API, YouTube Data API) they access the access tokens, basic profile information and video performance statistics of those accounts. We do not collect personal data from viewers, and this website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How we use it

Access tokens are used solely to upload our videos and read their statistics. Statistics are used to decide what to produce next. Tokens are stored encrypted on our own equipment and are never shared or sold.

Third parties

Videos we publish are subject to the privacy policies of the platform where you watch them. Some descriptions contain Amazon affiliate links; Amazon's privacy policy applies when you follow them.

YouTube API Services

Our tools use YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channels, manage their titles, captions, thumbnails and playlists, read and reply to comments on our own videos, and read our own channels' statistics. For topic research they also run a small number of YouTube searches and read the public view counts of the results; that public data is cached for at most 7 days and deleted from everything we store within 30 days, keeping only our own derived score. By using our YouTube channels you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to our tools can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected. You may revoke our access at any time from the settings of the respective platform.

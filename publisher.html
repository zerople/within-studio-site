Within Studio Publisher

Within Studio Publisher is the internal publishing tool our team uses to post finished episodes to the social accounts Within Studio owns. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

What it does

Connect an accountAn operator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) for one of our own accounts, @thewoodwithin_us or @thehousewithin_us, and approves access on TikTok's consent screen.
Show the accountThe tool shows the connected account's display name, follower and like counts and its list of videos, so the team can see what is already posted.
Publish a videoWhen an episode is approved, the operator posts it to the connected account, or uploads it as a draft to finish in the TikTok app. Captions come from the approved storyboard; every post is marked as AI-generated content.
DisconnectAccess can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or by asking us; stored tokens are then deleted.

Data

The tool stores only the access tokens and public statistics of our own accounts. It never collects data from viewers. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

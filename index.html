How a video is made

  1. Topic and research. We pick a question people are asking this week and collect the sources: manufacturer manuals, extension-service publications, safety agencies and peer-reviewed papers.
  2. Storyboard. Every scene is planned on paper first: what the viewer sees, the one line that is said, the camera, and the source behind each fact.
  3. Script and narration. The script is written to the storyboard, checked line by line against the sources, then narrated.
  4. Visuals. Scenes are produced as miniature-diorama footage with AI image and video tools, then reviewed against the storyboard. Every video is labeled as containing synthetic media.
  5. Review and publishing. A final review checks facts, timing and captions. Approved videos are published to our own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts with Within Studio Publisher.
